Your data

Everything this extension records stays in your browser. It is never sent anywhere — there is no server, no account, and no analytics.

aiU is a rough, directional unit — roughly 500 characters of AI output per unit, weighted by category. Click a legend item on either chart to toggle it on or off. Man-day figures assume ~3,000 characters/hour of researched writing and an 8-hour day; they model what the same volume of text might have cost to produce, not measured time saved. It's all meant to make an invisible pattern visible, not to be a precise measurement.
